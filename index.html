About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rice University, advised by Dr. Maryam Aliakbarpour. I work on problems at the intersection of theoretical computer science, statistical learning theory, and optimization.

My research focuses on designing algorithms with rigorous guarantees, particularly in settings involving limited information, predictions, privacy, or heterogeneous data.

Prior to joining Rice, I completed my Bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology.
